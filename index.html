Why now

Six reasons for
a new site now.

The old site was built some years ago on software that has since reached the end of its life. Here is what the new one needs to put right.

01

The site is offline

The domain lapsed on 1 October 2026, so anyone searching for you or typing in your web address finds nothing. Renewing it is the first job.

02

Software that is no longer supported

The old site ran on Joomla 3, which stopped receiving security updates in August 2023. Sites like it are being targeted by attackers right now.

03

Built for the phone in your pocket

Site managers check suppliers between jobs. Specs, photos and a tap-to-call button should be easy to use on a phone, not just a desktop.

04

Accreditations up front

CHAS, Constructionline, SafeContractor, CITB and FASET are what main contractors look for. They deserve a place on every page, not one at the bottom.

05

Product detail buyers can find

Leg heights of 1.5m and 1.8m, a 10.43kg panel, 2.0 kN/m² loading and three test standards. Clear spec panels answer questions before they are asked.

06

Found on Google again

Proper page titles, descriptions and a sitemap, registered with Google, so contractors searching for safety decking in the North West find you.
